I’m working toward a master’s in robotics in Europe. Robots need what I already do every day: motion that feels right, software that doesn’t break, and systems people can trust.

My first robotics project is a simulated rescue team: a drone and two ground robots that map a flooded Lagos street block without GPS, merge what they see into one map, and find a safe route for responders to reach a clinic.

The arm on this page is my warm-up. It’s a two-link arm solved with inverse kinematics, written from scratch in plain JavaScript and redrawn every frame as it follows you.
